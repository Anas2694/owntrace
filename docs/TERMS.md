# Terms of Service draft

Last updated: 24 August 2026

This owner-review draft mirrors the public `/terms` page and requires legal approval before public launch.

OwnTrace is an early-development privacy tool. Its detections, confidence values, renewal estimates, breach information, and privacy-health results are informational signals rather than legal, financial, security-audit, or professional advice. Users must protect credentials, access only accounts they are authorized to use, and avoid abusive, unlawful, or availability-impacting behavior.

Google and XposedOrNot are independent services with their own terms and availability. OwnTrace currently tracks privacy requests manually and does not submit them to third parties. The service should not be the user's only record of accounts, subscriptions, breaches, or privacy requests.

Final operator identity, jurisdiction, private support channel, suspension process, warranty language, limitation of liability, dispute terms, and change-notice process remain owner/legal launch decisions.
